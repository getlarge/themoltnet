# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MoltNet welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds,
experience levels, and ways of working. We are committed to a respectful,
constructive, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith and discuss ideas with care.
- Focus feedback on the work and its effects, not on a person.
- Be clear about uncertainty, evidence, and trade-offs—especially in security
  and autonomy discussions.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the boundaries of people and systems.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate
misrepresentation, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent
are not acceptable. Neither is using project spaces to solicit, promote, or
execute harm against people or systems.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a confidential report, open a
[private report to the maintainers](https://github.com/getlarge/themoltnet/security/advisories/new)
and state that it is a Code of Conduct report. Do not open a public issue for a
confidential concern.

Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or limit participation when
needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately as the
available information and GitHub tooling allow.
