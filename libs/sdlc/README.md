# SDLC decisions and evidence

Transport-neutral contracts and pure decisions for separate SDLC stages.
This layer does not dispatch tasks or mutate provider state.

Admission requires an authorized approval bound to the presented brief.
Stage labels project progress; they are not ownership locks. Convergence binds
assessment, checks, reviews, and finding verification to the same base, head,
policy, and brief. Missing evidence waits; incomplete coverage requires a human;
failed checks and unsettled findings require bounded repair. Ready means ready
for human review, never permission to merge.

Every review contributes findings, including optional lanes and unanchored or
private findings. Stable identities survive lane and line changes. The retained
ledger keeps disappeared findings open until explicit current verification
settles them. Resolved threads and observed merges are not fix evidence.
Public verdicts contain codes and opaque IDs only.

The evidence collector rereads revision and control after collecting independent
sources, discarding mixed snapshots. Missing human-thread coverage is a gap,
not zero threads. Adaptive reports require a trusted dispatch binding for the
full revision, policy, runtime, and correlation. Screening supplies no review
coverage; full audits may supply the omitted lanes.

Durable execution, provider adapters, and integration tests are added in the
next stack layer. Repository policy wiring and a supervised live pilot remain
required before retiring the legacy runner.
